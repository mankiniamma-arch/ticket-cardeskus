50-State Appeal FAQs

How do parking and camera ticket appeal deadlines vary across the 50 US states?
Contest windows vary by city and state—ranging from as short as 7 days in Chicago and 15 days in Seattle or Philadelphia, to 21 days in California and Massachusetts, and 30 days in NYC, Florida, Texas, and Washington, D.C. Always check the exact date printed on your ticket.
Which US states prohibit red-light or speed cameras?
Several states—including Texas (red-light cameras), Maine, Mississippi, Montana, New Hampshire, South Carolina, South Dakota, West Virginia, and Wisconsin—generally restrict or prohibit automated traffic cameras under state law, though local parking citations still apply.
